# CargoBeacon — Asset-Tracking Firmware & Apps (Consulting Engagement)

**Outcome in one line:** Multi-year consulting on an asset-tracking product line: a beacon tracker reaching **5+ years of battery life** (EFR32), **Wirepas mesh** firmware on nRF52840, and cross-platform companion apps — delivered with CI/CD and automated test suites.

**Client:** CargoBeacon AB (EU) — ongoing part-time consultancy
**Stack:** Silicon Labs EFR32, Nordic nRF52840, Wirepas Mesh, BLE, embedded C / Zephyr, Flutter, Rust, CI/CD
**Role:** Embedded consultant — firmware, apps, and engineering practices
**Timeline:** April 2021 – Present (parallel, part-time)

## What I've contributed

- **Ultra-low-power tracker firmware:** designed a beacon-based asset tracker on **Silicon Labs EFR32** (accelerometer, magnetic switch, LED, button) reaching **5+ years of battery life** — the low-power design that defines a fire-and-forget tag.
- **Wirepas mesh firmware:** developed **Wirepas Mesh** firmware on **nRF52840** across the protocol's node roles — mesh routing, positioning, ultra-low-power sleeping nodes, and secure provisioning. Specialist, hands-on Wirepas experience.
- **Cross-platform companion apps:** built tooling on **Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS** — **Flutter** first, later refactored around a **shared reusable Rust core** — with device interaction over **BLE and NFC** (firmware + app).
- **Engineering practices:** established **CI/CD** for automated builds and releases, and **automated + manual test suites** for the tracker firmware.

## What this demonstrates

- **Specialist Wirepas mesh expertise, ultra-low-power design (5+ years), cross-platform app development on a shared Rust core, and mature CI/CD + test engineering** — sustained across a multi-year client relationship.
